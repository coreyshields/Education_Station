# Week 08 - Counting Sets and Comparing Numbers

## Weekly Overview

This week focuses on number sense. The learner counts groups carefully, compares quantities, and uses math words in real situations.

## Goals

- Count objects accurately from 0-10 and begin oral counting beyond 10.
- Compare groups with more, less, same, one more, and one less.
- Recognize numerals 1-10 in mixed order.
- Explain simple counting choices out loud.
- Continue short read-aloud comprehension practice.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count from 1-10, then try 11-12 orally.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Counting Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count real objects and touch each once.
- 3 min: Say the total in a sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and answer "how many" when possible.
- 8 min: Color part of [Octopus Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Octopus.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count octopus arms or picture details.
- 3 min: Use "I counted..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count two groups of toys.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Comparing Numbers Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Comparing Numbers Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Say which group has more or less.
- 3 min: Add one object and say one more.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall one event.
- 7 min: Complete part of [Counting Shapes](<../../Math/Counting/Shapes.pdf>).
- 4 min: Sort counted shapes by type.
- 3 min: Review more, less, same.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Oral count to 13 or 14 with help.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Fish](<../../Math/Counting/Fish.pdf>).
- 5 min: Match counted amount to written numeral if possible.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "There are..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and find numbers or quantities.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 4 min: Point to numerals in random order.
- 3 min: Say favorite number.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 15 orally with support.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Crabs](<../../Math/Counting/Crabs.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare two picture groups.
- 3 min: Say same or different.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer a "where" question.
- 7 min: Finish [Octopus Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Octopus.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count bedtime objects up to 10.
- 3 min: One more and one less review.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 15, then count back from 5.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Fruit and Vegetables](<../../Math/Counting/Fruit and Vegetables.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare foods by size or amount.
- 3 min: Say, "The ___ has more."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one part.
- 7 min: Repeat a favorite counting worksheet.
- 4 min: Number hunt from 1-10 around the home.
- 3 min: Weekly review: count, compare, explain.
