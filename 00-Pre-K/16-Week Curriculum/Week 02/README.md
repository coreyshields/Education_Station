# Week 02 - T-S-F-I, Numbers 3-5, Circle

## Weekly Overview

This week deepens the first letter group while extending number recognition to 5. The learner also begins shape study with circles and continues line tracing for fine motor control.

## Goals

- Recognize T, S, F, and I in print and speech.
- Count and trace numerals 3, 4, and 5.
- Identify circles in the environment.
- Use more, less, same, and one more in everyday counting.
- Answer simple read-aloud questions with longer phrases.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count body movements from 1-5.
- 6 min: Introduce circles and find three circles nearby.
- 7 min: Complete part of [Circle](<../../Shapes/Circle.pdf>).
- 3 min: Say, "The circle is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "Who is in the story?"
- 8 min: Complete part of [T Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/T - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count round objects.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I found a circle."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review A, M, T, S, F, I.
- 8 min: Complete part of [3 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/3 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Build sets of 1, 2, and 3.
- 3 min: Compare which set has more.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and find a page with a circle shape.
- 8 min: Complete part of [S Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/S - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Draw circles in the air.
- 3 min: Ask, "What is bigger?"

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 3 min: Clap and count to 4.
- 8 min: Complete part of [4 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/4 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Put four toys in a row and count slowly.
- 4 min: Practice one more with 3 becoming 4.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "Where did they go?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [F Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/F - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Color part of [Beach Ball Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Beach Ball.pdf>).
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I like..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 5 using fingers.
- 8 min: Complete part of [5 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/5 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Make a set of five and remove one.
- 3 min: Say which set has less.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and recall one detail.
- 7 min: Complete part of [I Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/I - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Trace [Lines - 3](<../../Tracing Lines/Lines - 3.pdf>) with slow pencil control.
- 3 min: Review circle, more, and less.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Sing alphabet song and pause at T, S, F, I.
- 8 min: Finish favorite parts of number tracing 3-5.
- 5 min: Circle hunt around the home.
- 3 min: Tell one complete sentence about a circle.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened first?"
- 7 min: Finish [Beach Ball Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Beach Ball.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count five bedtime objects.
- 3 min: Weekly review: letters, numbers, shape.
