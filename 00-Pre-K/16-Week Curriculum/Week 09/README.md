# Week 09 - Shapes and Spatial Reasoning

## Weekly Overview

This week reviews all available shape worksheets and adds spatial reasoning through mazes, movement, and shape descriptions.

## Goals

- Identify circle, triangle, rectangle, oval, star, and trapezoid.
- Trace many shapes with better pencil control.
- Count sides, corners, or points when appropriate.
- Use spatial words: inside, outside, over, under, around, beside.
- Complete simple mazes with planning and finger-tracing first.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Shape song or shape flash review.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Trace Many Shapes](<../../Shapes/Trace Many Shapes.pdf>).
- 5 min: Name each shape before tracing.
- 3 min: Say, "This shape is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and find shapes in pictures.
- 8 min: Color part of [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count colors or arcs.
- 3 min: Use above and below.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Count corners on triangle and rectangle.
- 8 min: Complete part of [How many Angles](<../../Shapes/How many Angles.pdf>).
- 4 min: Build shapes with sticks or fingers.
- 3 min: Say how many corners.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "Where is it?"
- 7 min: Finger-trace [Back to School Maze](<../../Mazes/Back to School.pdf>) before using pencil.
- 4 min: Use around, through, and beside.
- 3 min: Celebrate careful path planning.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Sort household objects by shape.
- 8 min: Review [Circle](<../../Shapes/Circle.pdf>) and [Oval](<../../Shapes/Oval.pdf>) as needed.
- 5 min: Compare circle and oval.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "They are the same because..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and recall one setting.
- 8 min: Complete [Duck Maze](<../../Mazes/Duck.pdf>) or finger-trace it.
- 4 min: Count turns in the maze.
- 3 min: Use "inside" and "outside."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review rectangle, triangle, star.
- 8 min: Revisit [Triangle](<../../Shapes/Triangle.pdf>), [Rectangle](<../../Shapes/Rectangle.pdf>), or [Star](<../../Shapes/Star.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count sides or points.
- 3 min: Say, "A ___ has..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer who, what, and where.
- 7 min: Continue [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Place crayons above, below, and beside paper.
- 3 min: Review spatial words.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Shape hunt around the home.
- 8 min: Use favorite shape worksheet for review.
- 5 min: Sort shapes into groups.
- 3 min: Count how many in each group.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one part.
- 7 min: Finish [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Draw one circle, one triangle, and one rectangle.
- 3 min: Weekly review: shape name, sides, position.
