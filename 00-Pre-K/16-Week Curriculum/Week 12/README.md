# Week 12 - Listening Comprehension and Story Recall

## Weekly Overview

This week gives reading foundations extra attention. The learner listens, answers questions, compares stories, and practices speaking in complete thoughts.

## Goals

- Listen to short read-alouds with attention.
- Answer who, what, where, and what happened questions.
- Retell a beginning, middle, or end with support.
- Compare two stories or two characters.
- Continue light letter, number, and fine motor review.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Look at a book cover and predict.
- 8 min: Use [Listening and Answering](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Listening and Answering.pdf>).
- 5 min: Read a short book or section aloud.
- 3 min: Answer "who" and "what."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud another short book.
- 7 min: Trace part of [Lines 5](<../../Tracing Lines/Lines 5.pdf>).
- 4 min: Recall one event from the story.
- 3 min: Say, "In the story..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review beginning, middle, end with fingers.
- 8 min: Continue [Listening and Answering](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Listening and Answering.pdf>).
- 5 min: Ask where and why-style questions at a Pre-K level.
- 3 min: Practice answering in a phrase.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and choose a favorite page.
- 8 min: Color part of [Hedgehog Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Hedgehog.pdf>).
- 4 min: Describe the picture with color and size words.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "My favorite part is..."

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read or summarize two very short stories.
- 8 min: Use [Comparing Stories](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Comparing Stories.pdf>).
- 4 min: Name something same and different.
- 3 min: Use "both" in a sentence with help.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and compare to the morning story.
- 7 min: Use [Mystery Letter Game](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Mystery Letter Game.pdf>) for review.
- 4 min: Count letters recognized.
- 3 min: Name one story character.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Sound warm-up with familiar beginning sounds.
- 8 min: Use [Matching Sounds](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Matching Sounds.pdf>).
- 5 min: Match sounds to household objects.
- 3 min: Say, "___ starts with..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask who, what, where.
- 7 min: Continue [Hedgehog Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Hedgehog.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count to 18 orally with support.
- 3 min: Retell one thing from the day.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review story question words.
- 8 min: Finish or revisit [Comparing Stories](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Comparing Stories.pdf>).
- 5 min: Draw or act out one story event.
- 3 min: Say the event in a full sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell beginning, middle, or end.
- 7 min: Finish [Hedgehog Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Hedgehog.pdf>).
- 4 min: Letter and number quick review.
- 3 min: Weekly review: listening and answering.
