# Week 06 - V-K-Z-X-Q, Star and Trapezoid

## Weekly Overview

This week finishes broad A-Z exposure with less common letters and adds more complex shapes. The emphasis stays playful: noticing, sorting, tracing, and describing.

## Goals

- Recognize V, K, Z, X, and Q.
- Complete A-Z exposure through letter games and tracing.
- Identify stars and trapezoids as special shapes.
- Use positional language such as on, under, beside, above, and below.
- Continue counting and comparing sets to 10.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Alphabet song with pauses for V, K, Z, X, Q.
- 8 min: Use [Meet the Letters V-K-Z-X-Q](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters V-K-Z-X-Q.pdf>).
- 5 min: Trace V and X in the air.
- 3 min: Say, "The letter is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and name one object in the story.
- 8 min: Complete part of [V Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/V - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count objects up to 10.
- 3 min: Put one object above, below, and beside another.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Introduce star and count points.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Star](<../../Shapes/Star.pdf>).
- 4 min: Find stars in books, clothes, or drawings.
- 3 min: Say, "The star is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer "what happened?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [K Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/K - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Color part of [Astronaut Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Astronaut.pdf>).
- 3 min: Positional prompt: "The crayon is under..."

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Introduce trapezoid as a special four-sided shape.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Trapezoid](<../../Shapes/Trapezoid.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare rectangle and trapezoid.
- 3 min: Say same and different.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask a "where" question.
- 7 min: Complete part of [Z Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/Z - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Sort shapes from drawings or toys.
- 3 min: Count shape groups.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review all letters introduced so far.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Trace Many Shapes](<../../Shapes/Trace Many Shapes.pdf>).
- 5 min: Name each shape before tracing.
- 3 min: Use "on top" and "under."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall a favorite detail.
- 7 min: Complete part of [X Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/X - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Continue [Astronaut Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Astronaut.pdf>).
- 3 min: Describe the picture in a full sentence.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review V, K, Z, X, Q.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Q Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/Q - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Shape review: star, trapezoid, oval, circle, triangle, rectangle.
- 3 min: Count to 10.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer who, what, and where.
- 7 min: Finish [Astronaut Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Astronaut.pdf>).
- 4 min: A-Z letter hunt with favorite book cover.
- 3 min: Weekly celebration sentence.
