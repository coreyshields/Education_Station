# Week 01 - Routine, Lines, A-M, Numbers 0-2

## Weekly Overview

This week starts the Pre-K routine gently. The learner practices sitting for two short blocks, tracing lines, counting small groups, naming letters from the first alphabet set, and answering simple questions in complete phrases.

## Goals

- Follow a 20-minute morning and 20-minute evening learning rhythm.
- Trace straight and simple curved lines with growing control.
- Recognize and name A, M, T, S, F, and I.
- Count objects from 0-2 and begin recognizing numerals 1 and 2.
- Practice complete responses such as "I see a..." and "I want..."

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 3 min: Welcome song and count claps from 0-2.
- 5 min: Talk about morning, afternoon, and bedtime.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Lines - 1](<../../Tracing Lines/Lines - 1.pdf>).
- 4 min: Find A and M in the room or in a book.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened?"
- 5 min: Count two toys, then take one away and count again.
- 7 min: Color [Apple Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Apple.pdf>).
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I see a..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Alphabet song with extra attention to A, M, T.
- 8 min: Use [Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I.pdf>).
- 5 min: Trace A with finger, crayon, then pencil.
- 3 min: Say, "A is for apple" and name another A word.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Count steps, blocks, or snacks up to 2.
- 8 min: Complete part of [1 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/1 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare one toy and two toys using more and less.
- 3 min: Ask and answer, "What is that?"

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 3 min: Trace big lines in the air.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Lines - 2](<../../Tracing Lines/Lines - 2.pdf>).
- 5 min: Introduce S, F, and I from the letter set.
- 4 min: Sort two objects by color or size.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "Where is it?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [M Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/M - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count 0, 1, 2 with fingers.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I want..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count claps, taps, and stomps to 2.
- 8 min: Complete part of [2 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/2 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Play "show me one" and "show me two."
- 3 min: Review A, M, T, S, F, I.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and name characters.
- 7 min: Finish or revisit [Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare empty, one, and two objects.
- 3 min: Use the phrase "I have..."

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Review song: letters A, M, T, S, F, I and numbers 0-2.
- 8 min: Use [0-5 Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/0-5 Activity Time.pdf>) for only 0-2 items.
- 4 min: Practice tracing favorite lines again.
- 3 min: Celebrate one thing that felt easy.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and recall one favorite part.
- 7 min: Finish [Apple Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Apple.pdf>) or free draw A, M, and 2.
- 5 min: Count bedtime items: pillow, book, blanket.
- 3 min: Ask, "What did we learn this week?"
