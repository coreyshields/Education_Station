# Week 07 - Full Alphabet Review and Matching Sounds

## Weekly Overview

This week shifts from exposure to recognition. The learner reviews all letters, practices matching sounds playfully, and strengthens listening and speaking through short daily conversations.

## Goals

- Recognize many uppercase letters from A-Z.
- Match familiar beginning sounds to pictures or spoken words.
- Practice listening carefully and answering in full phrases.
- Continue fine motor tracing through letters and lines.
- Count and compare small sets during games.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Sing alphabet song slowly.
- 8 min: Use [Mystery Letter Game](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Mystery Letter Game.pdf>).
- 5 min: Choose five letters and match them to names or objects.
- 3 min: Say, "This letter is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened?"
- 7 min: Trace part of [Lines - 4](<../../Tracing Lines/Lines - 4.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count letters found on a page.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I found..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review letter sounds with A, B, C, D, F.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Matching Sounds](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Matching Sounds.pdf>).
- 5 min: Say beginning sounds for favorite objects.
- 3 min: Clap syllables in names.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and name one object beginning sound.
- 8 min: Color part of [Bee Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Bee.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count stripes, wings, or colors in the picture.
- 3 min: Say, "Bee begins with B."

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Review first two letter-group worksheets.
- 8 min: Revisit [Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters A-M-T-S-F-I.pdf>) or [Meet the Letters D-R-G-L-O](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters D-R-G-L-O.pdf>).
- 4 min: Sort known and tricky letters.
- 3 min: Name one tricky letter.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall beginning and end.
- 7 min: Air-write favorite letters.
- 4 min: Count to 10 forward and backward with help.
- 3 min: Conversation: "My favorite letter is..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Review remaining letter groups.
- 8 min: Revisit [Meet the Letters E-W-J-P-Y](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters E-W-J-P-Y.pdf>) or [Meet the Letters U-C-B-N-H](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters U-C-B-N-H.pdf>).
- 4 min: Match letter cards to worksheet letters.
- 3 min: Say each letter name.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "Where did it happen?"
- 8 min: Finish [Bee Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Bee.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count groups of 5 and 10.
- 3 min: Use "same" and "different."

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: A-Z chant.
- 8 min: Revisit [Meet the Letters V-K-Z-X-Q](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters V-K-Z-X-Q.pdf>).
- 5 min: Mystery letter bag or parent-written letter cards.
- 3 min: Say a complete sentence for one letter.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer who, what, and where.
- 7 min: Repeat favorite alphabet worksheet activity.
- 4 min: Count how many letters were recognized.
- 3 min: Weekly review: easiest and hardest letters.
