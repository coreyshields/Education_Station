# Week 10 - Addition With Pictures

## Weekly Overview

This week introduces addition as joining groups. The learner uses pictures, toys, snacks, and story language to understand "and" and "altogether."

## Goals

- Combine two small groups and count the total.
- Use language such as add, plus, and altogether informally.
- Tell simple math stories with objects.
- Continue counting totals up to 10.
- Connect addition practice to read-aloud and play.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count two groups of blocks.
- 8 min: Use [0-5 Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/0-5 Activity Time.pdf>) for small joining stories.
- 5 min: Add 1 object to a group and count altogether.
- 3 min: Say, "One more makes..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and count objects in one picture.
- 8 min: Color part of [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count decorations or colors.
- 3 min: Addition story: "I had 1, then got 1 more."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 10.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Counting Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Combine two picture groups verbally.
- 3 min: Use the word altogether.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened next?"
- 7 min: Use toys for 2 plus 1, 2 plus 2, and 3 plus 1.
- 4 min: Draw dots for one addition story.
- 3 min: Say total in a full sentence.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 10 and try 16 orally with help.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Monsters](<../../Math/Counting/Monsters.pdf>).
- 5 min: Add one more monster with a drawn mark or toy.
- 3 min: Count the new total.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and find two groups in an illustration.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 4 min: Match totals to numerals.
- 3 min: Review one more.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Add snack pieces: 2 and 1, 3 and 2.
- 8 min: Complete part of [6-10 Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/6-10 Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count totals carefully.
- 3 min: Compare totals.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer "how many" when possible.
- 7 min: Continue [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>).
- 4 min: Make a cupcake math story.
- 3 min: Say, "Altogether there are..."

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review add and altogether.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Fish](<../../Math/Counting/Fish.pdf>).
- 5 min: Tell two fish joining stories.
- 3 min: Count totals up to 10.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one part.
- 7 min: Finish [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>).
- 4 min: Play "add one more" with toys.
- 3 min: Weekly review: joining groups.
