# Week 05 - U-C-B-N-H, Counting Review, Oval

## Weekly Overview

This week reviews counting 0-10 while introducing another letter group. Oval work extends shape vocabulary and invites comparison with circles.

## Goals

- Recognize U, C, B, N, and H.
- Review numerals 0-10 through tracing, counting, and games.
- Identify ovals and compare ovals with circles.
- Describe familiar objects by shape, size, and color.
- Practice back-and-forth conversation during play.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count 0-10 with fingers and objects.
- 8 min: Use [Meet the Letters U-C-B-N-H](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters U-C-B-N-H.pdf>).
- 5 min: Find C and B in books or labels.
- 3 min: Say, "I found..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask one "where" question.
- 8 min: Complete part of [0-5 Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/0-5 Activity Time.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare two groups within 0-5.
- 3 min: Use "more" and "less."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Introduce oval and compare to circle.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Oval](<../../Shapes/Oval.pdf>).
- 4 min: Find oval-like objects or draw ovals.
- 3 min: Say, "An oval is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and describe one picture.
- 7 min: Complete part of [U Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/U - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Color part of [Avocado Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Avocado.pdf>).
- 3 min: Review U, C, B, N, H.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count 6-10 with blocks.
- 8 min: Complete part of [6-10 Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/6-10 Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare 6 and 10 using fewer and more.
- 3 min: Ask, "Which group has less?"

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and recall one action.
- 7 min: Complete part of [C Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/C - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Sort circles and ovals in drawings.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "This is round."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Number chant 1-10.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 5 min: Make a number line with cards or sticky notes.
- 3 min: Point to a requested number.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What do you see?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [B Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/B - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Continue [Avocado Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Avocado.pdf>).
- 3 min: Conversation: choose apple or avocado and explain.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review U, C, B, N, H.
- 8 min: Complete part of [N Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/N - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>) or [H Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/H - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Shape review: circle, oval, triangle, rectangle.
- 3 min: Say one complete shape sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one event.
- 7 min: Finish [Avocado Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Avocado.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count 10 bedtime objects.
- 3 min: Weekly review with favorite letter and shape.
