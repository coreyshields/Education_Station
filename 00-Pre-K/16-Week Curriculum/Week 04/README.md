# Week 04 - E-W-J-P-Y, Numbers 9-10, Rectangle

## Weekly Overview

This week completes confident counting to 10 and introduces a third letter group. Rectangle work builds shape vocabulary and everyday observation.

## Goals

- Recognize E, W, J, P, and Y.
- Count to 10 and trace numerals 9 and 10.
- Identify rectangles and compare them with circles and triangles.
- Retell one part of a read-aloud.
- Use descriptive language for color, size, and shape.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count from 1-10 while touching fingers.
- 8 min: Use [Meet the Letters E-W-J-P-Y](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters E-W-J-P-Y.pdf>).
- 5 min: Find E and P in print.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I see letter..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "Who was in the story?"
- 8 min: Complete part of [9 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/9 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count nine small objects.
- 3 min: Compare 9 to 8.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Introduce rectangle and count sides.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Rectangle](<../../Shapes/Rectangle.pdf>).
- 4 min: Find rectangles: door, book, table, screen.
- 3 min: Say, "The rectangle is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and point to a rectangle on a page if possible.
- 7 min: Complete part of [E Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/E - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Color part of [Penguin Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Penguin.pdf>).
- 3 min: Review E, W, J, P, Y.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 10 with claps.
- 8 min: Complete part of [10 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/10 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Make a group of 10 and split into two smaller groups.
- 3 min: Say total: "Together there are 10."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened first?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [W Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/W - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare rectangle, triangle, and circle.
- 3 min: Use "same" and "different."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review numbers 1-10.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count a mixed set slowly.
- 3 min: Name the last number counted as the total.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and recall one favorite part.
- 7 min: Complete part of [J Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/J - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Continue [Penguin Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Penguin.pdf>).
- 3 min: Describe the picture using color words.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Alphabet review for E, W, J, P, Y.
- 8 min: Complete part of [P Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/P - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>) or [Y Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/Y - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Shape walk: circle, triangle, rectangle.
- 3 min: Say one sentence for each shape.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened at the end?"
- 7 min: Finish [Penguin Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Penguin.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count to 10 one final time.
- 3 min: Weekly review: letters, numbers, rectangle.
