# Week 03 - D-R-G-L-O, Numbers 6-8, Triangle

## Weekly Overview

This week introduces a new letter group and expands counting to 8. Triangle work supports shape recognition, angle awareness, and descriptive language.

## Goals

- Recognize D, R, G, L, and O.
- Count groups up to 8 and trace numerals 6, 7, and 8.
- Identify triangles and describe them as having three sides or corners.
- Compare groups using more, less, and same.
- Practice asking and answering "where" and "what" questions.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 6 with jumps or taps.
- 8 min: Use [Meet the Letters D-R-G-L-O](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Meet the Letters D-R-G-L-O.pdf>).
- 5 min: Find D and O in books or labels.
- 3 min: Say, "I see the letter..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "Where is it?"
- 8 min: Complete part of [6 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/6 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count six toys and compare to five.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "Six is more than..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Introduce triangle and count sides.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Triangle](<../../Shapes/Triangle.pdf>).
- 4 min: Build triangles with craft sticks or fingers.
- 3 min: Say, "A triangle has..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and name one character action.
- 7 min: Complete part of [D Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/D - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Color part of [Dinosaur Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur.pdf>).
- 3 min: Review D, R, G, L, O.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 7 with fingers plus objects.
- 8 min: Complete part of [7 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/7 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare groups of 6 and 7.
- 3 min: Ask, "Which has more?"

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened next?"
- 7 min: Complete part of [R Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/R - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Triangle hunt in pictures or toys.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "I found..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 8 using slow touches.
- 8 min: Complete part of [8 Number Tracing](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/8 - Number Tracing.pdf>).
- 5 min: Make a set of 8, then one less.
- 3 min: Say how many remain.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and answer a "what" question.
- 7 min: Complete part of [G Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/G - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Continue [Dinosaur Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur.pdf>).
- 3 min: Review one more and one less.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review counting 0-8.
- 8 min: Complete part of [L Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/L - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 5 min: Sort objects into triangle and not triangle.
- 3 min: Say one full sentence about the week.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall beginning, middle, or end.
- 7 min: Finish [Dinosaur Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur.pdf>).
- 4 min: Review D, R, G, L, O with a letter hunt.
- 3 min: Count favorite objects up to 8.
