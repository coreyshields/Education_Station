# Pre-K 16-Week Curriculum

This 16-week plan turns the Pre-K guide into a practical home routine with two short learning blocks each day:

- Morning block: 20 minutes of new skill exposure, movement, conversation, and guided worksheet use.
- Evening block: 20 minutes of review, read-aloud conversation, playful math, art, or fine motor practice.

The plan is designed for five days per week. It covers the full Pre-K scope described in the main guide: counting from 0-20, numeral recognition, basic shapes, early comparison, playful addition and subtraction, measurement language, time awareness, A-Z letter exposure, listening comprehension, simple story recall, full-sentence speaking, and fine motor development.

## Weekly Scope

| Week | Focus | Education Goals Met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Week 01](<Week 01/README.md>) | Routine, lines, A-M, numbers 0-2 | Build lesson rhythm, trace lines, recognize first letter group, count small sets, answer simple questions. |
| [Week 02](<Week 02/README.md>) | T-S-F-I, numbers 3-5, circle | Trace early letters and numbers, count to 5, identify circles, speak in complete choice sentences. |
| [Week 03](<Week 03/README.md>) | D-R-G-L-O, numbers 6-8, triangle | Extend counting, recognize new letters, identify triangle features, compare more and less. |
| [Week 04](<Week 04/README.md>) | E-W-J-P-Y, numbers 9-10, rectangle | Count to 10, trace larger numerals, identify rectangles, retell parts of a story. |
| [Week 05](<Week 05/README.md>) | U-C-B-N-H, counting review, oval | Review numerals 0-10, strengthen letter recognition, describe objects by shape, size, and color. |
| [Week 06](<Week 06/README.md>) | V-K-Z-X-Q, star and trapezoid | Finish A-Z exposure, practice unusual letters, sort shapes, use positional language. |
| [Week 07](<Week 07/README.md>) | Full alphabet review and matching sounds | Match letters to sounds playfully, identify beginning sounds, practice full-sentence answers. |
| [Week 08](<Week 08/README.md>) | Counting sets and comparing numbers | Count objects accurately, compare groups, use more, less, same, one more, and one less. |
| [Week 09](<Week 09/README.md>) | Shapes and spatial reasoning | Identify and trace shapes, count sides and angles, describe position and movement. |
| [Week 10](<Week 10/README.md>) | Addition with pictures | Combine small groups, tell simple math stories, count totals up to 10. |
| [Week 11](<Week 11/README.md>) | Subtraction with pictures | Take away objects, count what remains, explain simple everyday subtraction. |
| [Week 12](<Week 12/README.md>) | Listening comprehension and story recall | Listen to read-alouds, compare stories, answer who, what, where, and what happened questions. |
| [Week 13](<Week 13/README.md>) | Three-letter words and sound play | Blend simple sounds orally, recognize short words, connect letters to familiar words. |
| [Week 14](<Week 14/README.md>) | Measurement, time, and daily routines | Use big, small, heavy, light, tall, short, morning, afternoon, evening, and bedtime. |
| [Week 15](<Week 15/README.md>) | Integrated review | Review letters, numbers, shapes, counting, story recall, and fine motor skills. |
| [Week 16](<Week 16/README.md>) | Celebration and readiness check | Demonstrate confidence with Pre-K skills through games, conversation, art, and favorite worksheets. |

## Core Teaching Pattern

Each day uses a simple rhythm that can stay familiar while the skills rotate:

1. Warm up with song, counting, movement, or conversation.
2. Teach or review one focused skill.
3. Use one worksheet or hands-on activity.
4. Close with a sentence prompt, short read-aloud question, or playful review.

Keep the mood light. At this stage, confidence and repetition matter more than perfect output.
