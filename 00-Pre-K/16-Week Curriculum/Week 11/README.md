# Week 11 - Subtraction With Pictures

## Weekly Overview

This week introduces subtraction as taking away. The learner uses toys, snacks, and pictures to count what remains after an object leaves a group.

## Goals

- Take away from small groups and count what remains.
- Use language such as take away, left, fewer, and one less.
- Tell simple subtraction stories.
- Continue counting up to 10 and oral counting toward 20.
- Practice calm problem solving through mazes and fine motor work.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count five objects.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Take one away and count what is left.
- 3 min: Say, "There are ___ left."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask, "What went away?"
- 8 min: Color part of [Carrot Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Carrot.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count carrots or colors.
- 3 min: One less with fingers.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 10, then count back from 5 with help.
- 8 min: Use [Comparing Numbers Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Comparing Numbers Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare before and after taking away.
- 3 min: Use fewer.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall one problem in the story.
- 7 min: Finger-trace [Cat Maze](<../../Mazes/Cat.pdf>), then complete if ready.
- 4 min: Talk through turns using around and through.
- 3 min: Celebrate careful planning.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Make a group of 6 and remove 1 or 2.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Chicken Directions](<../../Math/Counting/Chicken Directions.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count remaining objects after one leaves.
- 3 min: Say, "One less than ___ is ___."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and answer "how many are left?" if possible.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 4 min: Point to 1-10 in random order.
- 3 min: Count back from 3.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Snack subtraction with 3-5 pieces.
- 8 min: Continue [Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Tell subtraction stories.
- 3 min: Use "left" in a full sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask, "What happened at the end?"
- 7 min: Continue [Carrot Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Carrot.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare big and small carrots if drawn.
- 3 min: Review take away.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 17 orally with support.
- 8 min: Repeat favorite subtraction problems with toys.
- 5 min: Draw a group, cross one out, count left.
- 3 min: Say one subtraction story.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one part.
- 7 min: Finish [Carrot Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Carrot.pdf>).
- 4 min: Review one more and one less.
- 3 min: Weekly review: taking away.
