# Week 14 - Measurement, Time, and Daily Routines

## Weekly Overview

This week applies math and language to real life. The learner compares size and weight, talks about daily time words, and uses counting during routines.

## Goals

- Use measurement words: big, small, tall, short, long, heavy, light.
- Use time words: morning, afternoon, evening, bedtime, before, after.
- Count orally to 20 with support.
- Apply comparison language to toys, food, books, and family routines.
- Practice full-sentence choices and explanations.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Talk through the day: morning, afternoon, evening, bedtime.
- 8 min: Use [Comparing Numbers Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Comparing Numbers Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Compare big and small groups.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "This has more."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and talk about before and after.
- 7 min: Color part of [Beach Ball Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Beach Ball.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare colors or sections.
- 3 min: Say what happens at bedtime.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Compare tall and short objects.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Shapes](<../../Math/Counting/Shapes.pdf>).
- 5 min: Line up objects from shortest to tallest.
- 3 min: Say, "This is taller than..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and find something big or small in the pictures.
- 7 min: Use [Trace Many Shapes](<../../Shapes/Trace Many Shapes.pdf>) for careful tracing.
- 4 min: Count to 20 orally with help.
- 3 min: Review tall, short, big, small.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Compare heavy and light safe objects.
- 8 min: Complete part of [Counting Fruit and Vegetables](<../../Math/Counting/Fruit and Vegetables.pdf>).
- 5 min: Sort foods or toys by heavy and light.
- 3 min: Say, "The ___ is heavier."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and ask "what happened first?"
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count backward from 5 with help.
- 3 min: Talk about what comes after dinner.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review before and after with daily events.
- 8 min: Create a three-step routine picture or verbal sequence.
- 5 min: Put events in order: wake, eat, play, sleep.
- 3 min: Say, "Before ___, I..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and sequence two events.
- 7 min: Continue [Beach Ball Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Beach Ball.pdf>).
- 4 min: Compare long and short lines drawn on paper.
- 3 min: Use long and short in sentences.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 20 orally with support.
- 8 min: Repeat favorite comparison worksheet.
- 5 min: Real-world measurement hunt.
- 3 min: Say one measurement sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and talk about morning or bedtime in the story.
- 7 min: Finish [Beach Ball Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Beach Ball.pdf>).
- 4 min: Review time words.
- 3 min: Weekly review: measure and sequence.
