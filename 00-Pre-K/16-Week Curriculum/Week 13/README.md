# Week 13 - Three-Letter Words and Sound Play

## Weekly Overview

This week keeps phonics playful. The learner hears and says sounds, notices simple three-letter words, and connects letters to familiar spoken words.

## Goals

- Recognize that letters represent sounds.
- Play with beginning, middle, and ending sounds orally.
- Explore simple three-letter words without pressure to read independently.
- Continue tracing selected letters that need practice.
- Count orally toward 20 and review numbers 1-10.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Say and clap simple words like cat, sun, dog.
- 8 min: Use [3 Letter Words](<../../Reading/Activity Time/3 Letter Words.pdf>).
- 5 min: Stretch sounds orally: c-a-t.
- 3 min: Say, "The word is..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and find a short word.
- 7 min: Complete part of [A Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/A - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count to 19 orally with support.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "A is in..."

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Beginning sound game with toys.
- 8 min: Use [Matching Sounds](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Matching Sounds.pdf>).
- 5 min: Sort two objects by same or different starting sound.
- 3 min: Say, "They sound the same."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and answer "what happened?"
- 8 min: Color part of [Dinosaur 2 Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur 2.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count picture details.
- 3 min: Say a D sound word.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Review cat, hat, sun, dog as spoken words.
- 8 min: Continue [3 Letter Words](<../../Reading/Activity Time/3 Letter Words.pdf>).
- 5 min: Build a word with letter cards if available.
- 3 min: Point to first letter.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and recall one character.
- 7 min: Complete part of [C Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/C - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count 10 objects and trace numeral 10 in the air.
- 3 min: Say, "C is for..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Mystery beginning sound.
- 8 min: Use [Mystery Letter Game](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Mystery Letter Game.pdf>).
- 5 min: Match three known letters to sounds.
- 3 min: Say, "This starts with..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask where.
- 7 min: Complete part of [T Letter Tracing](<../../Reading/Tracing Letters/T - Letter Tracing Alphabet.pdf>).
- 4 min: Continue [Dinosaur 2 Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur 2.pdf>).
- 3 min: Say a T sound word.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 20 orally with help.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 5 min: Review three-letter words by listening and repeating.
- 3 min: Choose a favorite word.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one event.
- 7 min: Finish [Dinosaur 2 Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Dinosaur 2.pdf>).
- 4 min: Letter sound review.
- 3 min: Weekly review: sounds and short words.
