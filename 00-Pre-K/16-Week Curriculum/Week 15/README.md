# Week 15 - Integrated Review

## Weekly Overview

This week combines the full Pre-K scope into quick, playful review. Each day touches reading, math, language, and fine motor skills.

## Goals

- Review A-Z recognition and familiar beginning sounds.
- Review counting 0-20 orally and numerals 1-10 in print.
- Review shapes, comparison, addition, and subtraction stories.
- Retell story events and answer simple questions.
- Build confidence by repeating favorite worksheets.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: A-Z song and letter card review.
- 8 min: Use [Mystery Letter Game](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Mystery Letter Game.pdf>).
- 5 min: Match five letters to sounds.
- 3 min: Say a full sentence with a chosen letter.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer who, what, where.
- 7 min: Color part of [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count colors or sections.
- 3 min: Say favorite part of the story.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 20 with help.
- 8 min: Use [Counting Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Counting Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Match totals to numerals 1-10.
- 3 min: Say, "There are..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 5 min: Read aloud and find a number or quantity.
- 8 min: Finger-trace and complete [Bee Maze](<../../Mazes/Bee.pdf>).
- 4 min: Use around, through, beside.
- 3 min: Review careful pencil control.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Shape review.
- 8 min: Use [Trace Many Shapes](<../../Shapes/Trace Many Shapes.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count sides, corners, or points.
- 3 min: Say, "A ___ has..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one event.
- 7 min: Use [Matching Sounds](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Matching Sounds.pdf>).
- 4 min: Name beginning sounds for three objects.
- 3 min: Sentence prompt: "This starts with..."

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Compare two groups of toys.
- 8 min: Use [Comparing Numbers Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Comparing Numbers Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Add one more and take one away.
- 3 min: Use more, less, and same.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and answer "what happened next?"
- 7 min: Continue [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Review morning, afternoon, evening, bedtime.
- 3 min: Say what happens before bed.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 20 and review numbers 1-10.
- 8 min: Use [Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Subtraction With Pictures Activity Time.pdf>) for simple review.
- 5 min: Tell one addition and one subtraction story.
- 3 min: Say what is left or altogether.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and use [Listening and Answering](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Listening and Answering.pdf>) prompts.
- 7 min: Finish [Rainbow Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Rainbow.pdf>).
- 4 min: Choose favorite letter, number, and shape.
- 3 min: Weekly review: all Pre-K skills.
