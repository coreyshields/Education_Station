# Week 16 - Celebration and Readiness Check

## Weekly Overview

This final week is a gentle readiness check and celebration. The learner shows what they can do through favorite worksheets, games, conversation, read-alouds, and creative work.

## Goals

- Demonstrate confidence with many A-Z letters.
- Count orally toward 20 and count objects up to 10 or more with support.
- Recognize numerals 1-10 and common shapes.
- Use comparison, addition, subtraction, measurement, and time words in daily talk.
- Answer read-aloud questions and retell simple story moments.
- Complete fine motor work with improved control.

## Daily Agenda

### Day 1

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: A-Z song and name favorite letters.
- 8 min: Use [Mystery Letter Game](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Mystery Letter Game.pdf>).
- 5 min: Letter hunt in a book or room.
- 3 min: Say three letter sentences.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and ask who, what, where.
- 7 min: Color part of [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>).
- 4 min: Count and compare picture details.
- 3 min: Say what felt easy.

### Day 2

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Count to 20 with support.
- 8 min: Use [Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10](<../../Math/Tracing Numbers/Practice Numbers Tracing 1-10.pdf>).
- 5 min: Count objects and match totals to numerals.
- 3 min: Say, "I counted..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and retell one part.
- 7 min: Finger-trace and complete [Dinosaur Maze](<../../Mazes/Dinosaur.pdf>).
- 4 min: Use over, under, around, through.
- 3 min: Celebrate careful work.

### Day 3

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Shape flash review.
- 8 min: Use [Trace Many Shapes](<../../Shapes/Trace Many Shapes.pdf>).
- 5 min: Name shapes and count sides or points.
- 3 min: Say one shape sentence.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and compare two characters or events.
- 7 min: Use [3 Letter Words](<../../Reading/Activity Time/3 Letter Words.pdf>) for playful review.
- 4 min: Say beginning sounds.
- 3 min: Choose a favorite word.

### Day 4

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Addition and subtraction with toys.
- 8 min: Use [Counting Pictures Activity Time](<../../Math/Activity Time/Counting Pictures Activity Time.pdf>).
- 5 min: Tell one joining story and one taking-away story.
- 3 min: Say altogether or left.

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Read aloud and use [Listening and Answering](<../../Reading/Activity Time/Listening and Answering.pdf>) prompts.
- 7 min: Continue [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>).
- 4 min: Talk about morning, afternoon, evening, bedtime.
- 3 min: Say what happens after this lesson.

### Day 5

**Morning - 20 minutes**

- 4 min: Learner choice warm-up: letters, numbers, or shapes.
- 8 min: Repeat the favorite worksheet from the 16-week plan.
- 5 min: Parent observes confidence, attention, pencil control, and language.
- 3 min: Learner says, "I learned..."

**Evening - 20 minutes**

- 6 min: Favorite read-aloud and story retell.
- 7 min: Finish [Cupcake Coloring](<../../Art/Coloring/Cupcake.pdf>) or free draw.
- 4 min: Review favorite letter, number, shape, and story.
- 3 min: Celebrate completion of the 16-week Pre-K curriculum.
